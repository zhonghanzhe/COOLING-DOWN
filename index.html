关于我

Operating across commercial design and computational art, my practice spans from high-density visual communication in Shenzhen to real-time audiovisual and interactive systems in London. I am drawn to the liminal state of "post-forging"—examining how objects, images, and algorithmic systems slowly cool, develop, and settle after the intense heat of shaping.

创作实践横跨商业设计与计算艺术：从深圳高密度的商业视觉传播，延伸至伦敦的实时音画与交互系统。我着眼于一种「后锻造」的临界状态——观察物体、图像与算法系统在经历高温熔炼与塑形后，如何在室温中缓慢冷却、显影并最终沉淀。
